OTP Security Guidance

Banks should never ask customers to disclose their OTP to another person.

An OTP is intended to authenticate a transaction or login action initiated by the customer.

Messages asking customers to share, send, or provide an OTP should be treated as suspicious.

Urgent messages threatening account blocking while requesting an OTP are common characteristics of phishing and social engineering attacks.

Investigators should examine the wording of the message, the presence of links, and whether the customer was instructed to disclose authentication information.
